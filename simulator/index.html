① 为什么需要这个指标① Why this metric exists Why this metric exists

真实世界证据（real-world evidence, RWE）越来越多地进入指南与监管决策。研究者用目标试验模拟（target trial emulation, TTE）、TMLE 等方法，尽可能模仿随机试验。但这些方法共同依赖一个关键假设：无未测量混杂假设（no unmeasured confounding）——即"所有影响治疗与结局的共同原因都已被测量并调整"。 Real-world evidence (RWE) increasingly informs guidelines and regulatory decisions. Researchers use target trial emulation (TTE), TMLE, and related methods to mimic randomized trials as closely as possible. But all of these methods share one critical assumption: no unmeasured confounding — that "every common cause of treatment and outcome has been measured and adjusted for."

一项调查数据：对 237 项 TTE 研究的横断面调查显示，只有 30.8% 的研究用统计方法评估了未测量混杂（Ren et al., JAMA Network Open 2026）。也就是说，近七成的 TTE 研究没有任何经验性的偏倚检查就发表了因果结论。 A survey finding: a cross-sectional survey of 237 TTE studies found that only 30.8% used statistical methods to assess unmeasured confounding (Ren et al., JAMA Network Open 2026). In other words, nearly seven in ten TTE studies published causal conclusions without any empirical bias check.

健康依从者效应：药物流行病学中的典型混杂现象The healthy adherer effect: a canonical confounding mechanism in pharmacoepidemiology

健康依从者效应（healthy adherer effect）：依从治疗的患者，即使吃的是安慰剂，结局也更好——因为他们本身更健康、更自律、医疗资源更好。观察性研究中，"依从性好，按要求服药"这件事本身就携带了大量与药物无关的预后信息。 The healthy adherer effect: patients who adhere to therapy have better outcomes even on placebo — because they are healthier, more disciplined, and have better access to care to begin with. In observational studies, "taking the full dose" itself carries a wealth of prognostic information that has nothing to do with the drug.

It rests on three sequential concepts: negative control → the distribution of systematic bias (μ̂B, σ̂B) → calibrated p-value. BF and BER stand on these shoulders — see Animation D below.

第一步：什么是负对照（negative control）？ Step 1: What is a negative control?

负对照是一个治疗"不可能"影响的结局，但它和主结局共享同一套混杂结构（同一批未测量的因素 U 同时污染它和主结局）。因为真实效应已知为零，所以一旦观察到"治疗与负对照相关"，这段相关只能来自偏倚，绝不可能来自药物作用，这相当于一块"已知真值为 0 的校准砝码"：我们确切知道治疗对它没有真实作用，于是测量出的任何非零关联，都是天平（研究设计）的系统误差读数。 A negative control is an outcome the treatment cannot possibly affect, yet it shares the primary outcome's confounding structure (the same unmeasured factors U corrupt both). Because its true effect is known to be zero, any observed treatment–control association must come from bias, never from the drug — it is a "calibration weight known to be exactly 0": we know the treatment has no real effect on it, so any non-zero measured association is the scale's (the study design's) systematic-error reading.

🩺 举例：β 受体阻滞剂不可能预防白内障（cataract），但如果观察数据里"达标组"的白内障发生率也更低（比如：3.2% vs 4.2%，RR≈0.76）。β 受体阻滞剂理论上没法保护眼睛，所以这 0.76 的"保护作用"只能来自健康依从者效应这类混杂，它正是我们要校准的偏倚的直接证据。像这样的负对照我们可以寻找很多个，比如：β 受体阻滞剂 与 痢疾、β 受体阻滞剂 与 骨折等等。 🩺 Example: a beta-blocker cannot prevent cataract, yet in observational data the "target-dose" group also shows lower cataract rates (for example, 3.2% vs 4.2%, RR≈0.76). The drug cannot plausibly protect the eyes, so this 0.76 "protective effect" can only come from confounding such as the healthy-adherer effect, which is direct evidence of the very bias we must calibrate. We can find many such negative controls, for example beta-blocker versus dysentery or beta-blocker versus fracture.

第二步：估计"系统偏倚的分布"（μ̂B 与 σ̂B） Step 2: Estimate the "distribution of systematic bias" (μ̂B and σ̂B)

把 K 个负对照各自的估计值交给软件包，它会用最大似然拟合成一个钟形分布（经验零分布）。这个钟形有两个参数：
• 中心 μ̂B（平均系统偏倚）：设计平均把估计往哪边、推了多少，这相当于那台秤"平均偏低 0.3 kg"的恒定偏移量。
• 宽度 σ̂B（偏倚异质性）：不同负对照之间偏倚的离散程度，相当于同一块 0 kg 砝码每天复称，读数在 −0.2 到 −0.4 之间晃动的范围。
在我们的 β 受体阻滞剂研究里，12 个负对照全部偏向保护一侧，拟合出 μ̂B = −0.190（log RR 尺度，即设计平均把估计往"保护"方向推了 0.190），σ̂B = 0.068（各对照间偏倚的波动不大、彼此相当一致）。 Feed the K control estimates to the package and it fits them, by maximum likelihood, into a bell-shaped distribution (the empirical null). It has two parameters:
• Center μ̂B (mean systematic bias): on average, which way and how far the design pushes estimates, like the scale's constant offset of "running ~0.3 kg low."
• Width σ̂B (bias heterogeneity): how much the bias scatters across different controls, like the same 0 kg weight reweighed daily, readings wobbling between −0.2 and −0.4.
In our beta-blocker study, all 12 controls leaned protective, fitting μ̂B = −0.190 (log-RR scale, i.e. the design on average pushes estimates 0.190 toward "protection") and σ̂B = 0.068 (the bias is modest and fairly consistent across controls).

第三步：把观测估计放进"偏倚分布"里重新检验 → 校准 p 值 Step 3: Re-test the observed estimate against the "bias distribution" → the calibrated p-value

普通的 p 值检验的是："在没有偏倚的假设下（一个以 0 为中心的钟形），观测估计是否显著不等于 0？"而校准 p 值换了一个参照系：它检验的是"在数据揭示的实际偏倚分布（以 μ̂B 为中心、并纳入 σ̂B 不确定性的钟形）里，观测估计是否显著不等于 0？"换句话说，就是把判决的尺子，从"理想情形"换成"这台秤实际偏了多少"。 An ordinary p-value asks: "assuming no bias (a bell centered at 0), is the observed estimate significantly different from 0?" The calibrated p-value swaps the reference frame: it asks "against the bias distribution the data actually reveal (a bell centered at μ̂B, with the uncertainty σ̂B), is the observed estimate significantly different from 0?" In short, it swaps the yardstick of the verdict from "the ideal case" to "how far this scale actually strays."

🩺 临床比喻：普通 p 值相当于问"这个病人的体重，和 70 kg 教科书标准值差得多吗"；校准 p 值相当于问"这个病人的体重，和我们这台已知偏低 0.3 kg 的秤通常会显示的值差得多吗"。本例主结局测得 log RR = −0.186（未校准 RR≈0.83）：若按"无偏倚"的尺子，它显著不等于 0；但放进以 −0.190 为中心的偏倚钟形里，它恰好落在正中央，说明这段"差异"和秤自身偏差几乎一模一样。于是校准 p 值 = 0.94，结论是"扣除秤的偏差后，没有证据显示真实效应存在"。注意：这里的"没证据"恰恰是因为偏倚把估计从真值附近推到了偏倚中心，而偏倚本身很大，却被悄悄归了零。 🩺 Clinical analogy: the ordinary p-value asks "is this patient's weight far from the 70 kg textbook value?"; the calibrated p-value asks "is this patient's weight far from what our scale known to read 0.3 kg low would show?" Here the primary outcome reads log RR = −0.186 (uncalibrated RR≈0.83): against the "no-bias" yardstick it is significantly ≠ 0; but placed in the bias bell centered at −0.190 it lands dead center, meaning this "difference" is almost identical to the scale's own error. So the calibrated p-value = 0.94 and the verdict is "after removing the scale's bias, no evidence of a real effect." Note: this "no evidence" is precisely because the bias pushed the estimate from near the truth to the bias center, yet the bias itself is large and got silently zeroed out.

校准 p 值到底在说什么？（划重点）What the calibrated p-value actually says (key point)
  • 校准 p > 0.05：扣除系统偏倚之后，剩下的效应小到与"零"没有统计上可区分的差别 → "没有证据表明该治疗存在真实效应"。注意：这绝不表示"没有偏倚"。恰恰相反，此时偏倚往往很大——正是它把估计"拨"到了零附近。Calibrated p > 0.05: after removing systematic bias, the residual effect is too small to distinguish from zero → "no evidence of a real treatment effect." Note: this does NOT mean "no bias." Quite the opposite — the bias is often large at this point; it is exactly what pulled the estimate toward zero.
  • 校准 p < 0.05：扣除偏倚之后，仍然有与零可区分的残余效应 → "有证据支持真实效应"。Calibrated p < 0.05: after removing bias, a residual effect distinguishable from zero remains → "evidence supports a real effect."
它的局限：这是一个"是 / 否"的二元判决，回答不了下一个问题——"扣除的偏倚有多大？剩下的效应又有多大？" 两个截然不同的情形会给出同一个"不显著"： Its limitation: it is a yes/no verdict and cannot answer the next question — "how large was the bias removed, and how large is the effect that remains?" Two very different scenarios yield the same "not significant":
"校准后不显著"的两种含义：
① 效应被偏倚淹没——真实效应存在，但偏倚太大，校准后信号被抹平；
② 本来就无效——校准正确地移除了偏倚，剩下一个真实的零效应。
校准 p 值无法区分这两种情况。BF——本论文提出的连续量——正是为区分它们而设计：它直接报告"偏倚占校准后信号的几成"。 Two meanings of "not significant after calibration":
① The effect was swamped by bias — a real effect exists, but the bias was so large that the calibrated signal vanished;
② Truly null — calibration correctly removed the bias, leaving a genuine zero effect.
The calibrated p-value cannot distinguish the two. BF — the continuous metric introduced by this paper — is designed precisely to separate them: it directly reports "what share of the calibrated signal is bias."

负对照结局Negative control outcome
严谨定义：与主结局共享同一套混杂结构、但不可能被治疗因果影响的结局。若治疗与负对照也出现关联，该关联只能来自混杂（或偏倚）。 Formal definition: an outcome that shares the confounding structure of the primary outcome but cannot be causally affected by treatment. If treatment associates with a negative control, that association can only arise from confounding (bias).
💡 通俗解释：负对照的真实效应已知为零。若治疗与负对照也出现关联，该关联不可能来自药物作用，只能来自研究设计中的系统偏倚。 💡 Plain language: a negative control's true effect is known to be zero. Any treatment–control association cannot come from the drug — it can only come from systematic bias in the study design.
经验零分布Empirical null distribution
严谨定义：用 K 个负对照的估计值 (γ̂k, τ̂k) 通过最大似然拟合的分布 N( μ̂B, √τ̂k² + σ̂B² )，其中 μ̂B 是平均系统偏倚，σ̂B 是对照间偏倚的异质性。 Formal definition: the distribution N( μ̂B, √τ̂k² + σ̂B² ) fitted by maximum likelihood to the K negative-control estimates (γ̂k, τ̂k), where μ̂B is the mean systematic bias and σ̂B the between-control heterogeneity of bias.
💡 通俗解释：负对照集合刻画了偏倚的分布特征。12 个负对照全部偏向保护一侧，说明研究设计会系统性地高估保护作用。 💡 Plain language: the control set characterizes the distribution of bias. All 12 controls leaning protective means the study design systematically over-estimates protective effects.
偏倚交换性Bias exchangeability
严谨定义：在给定已测协变量的条件下，影响主估计的残留偏倚的分布 ≈ 支配负对照估计的偏倚分布。这是整个方法的关键假设，与它所诊断的"无未测量混杂"一样无法用数据直接验证。 Formal definition: conditional on measured covariates, the distribution of residual bias affecting the primary estimate ≈ the bias distribution governing the negative-control estimates. This is the key assumption of the whole method and, like the "no unmeasured confounding" it diagnoses, cannot be verified directly from data.
💡 通俗比喻："同一把尺子"假设——负对照和主结局被同一套混杂机制污染。如果负对照的偏倚是"尺子 A"造成的，而主结局的偏倚来自"尺子 B"，校准就会用错尺子。 💡 Analogy: the "same ruler" assumption — the controls and the primary outcome are contaminated by one and the same confounding mechanism. If the controls' bias comes from "ruler A" while the primary outcome's bias comes from "ruler B," calibration uses the wrong ruler.

BF 与 BER 是怎么"站在负对照的肩膀上"的（一句话总结）： OHDSI 的 EmpiricalCalibration 包（Schuemie 等的已有方法与软件）解决的是"有没有偏倚"（μ̂B）和"扣除偏倚后还剩不剩效应"（校准 p 值，二元判决）。BF 与 BER 是本论文提出的两个新指标，它们不在 EmpiricalCalibration 包的输出之列，而是把包已经算出的两个量——μ̂B（偏倚）与 ψ̃ = ψ̂obs − μ̂B（校准后残留效应）——做成一个比值，从而对已有框架形成强化与扩展：BF = |μ̂B| / (|μ̂B| + |ψ̃|)（偏倚占校准后信号的几成，有界 0–1）、BER = |μ̂B| / |ψ̃|（偏倚是残留效应的几倍，无界）。这样就把"是/否"的判决，扩展成了对未测量偏倚的连续量化：偏倚有多大、剩下的效应有多大、二者谁占主导。 How BF and BER "stand on the shoulders of" negative controls (one-sentence summary): The OHDSI EmpiricalCalibration package (pre-existing methodology and software by Schuemie et al.) answers "is there bias" (μ̂B) and "does any effect survive calibration" (the calibrated p-value, a binary verdict). BF and BER are two new metrics introduced by this paper — they are not outputs of the EmpiricalCalibration package. They strengthen and extend the existing framework by turning two quantities the package already computes — μ̂B (bias) and ψ̃ = ψ̂obs − μ̂B (the calibrated residual effect) — into ratios: BF = |μ̂B| / (|μ̂B| + |ψ̃|) (bias's share of the calibrated signal, bounded 0–1) and BER = |μ̂B| / |ψ̃| (bias as a multiple of the residual effect, unbounded). The yes/no verdict is thereby extended into a continuous quantification of unmeasured bias: how large the bias is, how large the remaining effect is, and which dominates.

④ 指标词典④ The metrics, defined and demystified The metrics, defined and demystified

每个指标给出三件套：严谨定义 → 数学符号 → 通俗解释（含比喻）。所有指标都只需负对照估计与主估计两个输入，无需额外数据。 Each metric comes as a triple: formal definition → mathematical notation → plain-language explanation (with an analogy). All metrics need only two inputs — the negative-control estimates and the primary estimate — and no additional data.

① 偏倚分数 Bias Fraction (BF) —— 主指标（本论文提出）① Bias Fraction (BF) — the primary metric (introduced by this paper)
严谨定义：在"校准后总信号 = 偏倚 + 残留效应"中，系统偏倚所占的份额： Formal definition: within the "calibrated total signal = bias + residual effect", the share attributable to systematic bias:
BF = |μ̂B| / ( |μ̂B| + |ψ̃| )  ∈ [0, 1]
BF = 1 ⇔ 校准后效应恰为 0（信号完全由偏倚构成）；BF = 0 ⇔ 未估计到任何系统偏倚。BF 与偏倚效应比一一对应：BF = BER / (1 + BER)。 BF = 1 ⇔ the calibrated effect is exactly 0 (the signal is entirely bias); BF = 0 ⇔ no systematic bias was estimated. BF is one-to-one with the bias-effect ratio: BF = BER / (1 + BER).
🍰 通俗解释（蛋糕比喻）：校准后剩下的信号是一块蛋糕，偏倚和残留效应各切一块。BF 就是"偏倚切走了几成"。BF = 0.91 意思是：这块蛋糕的九成一是偏倚，真正的"效应"只剩不到一成。BF 有界，永远不会爆出"无穷大"这种尴尬数字。 🍰 Plain language (cake analogy): the surviving calibrated signal is a cake, split between bias and residual effect. BF is "bias's slice of the cake." BF = 0.91 means 91% of the cake is bias and the true "effect" gets less than a tenth. BF is bounded — it will never blow up into an embarrassing "infinity".
② 偏倚效应比 Bias-Effect Ratio (BER) —— 辅助指标（本论文提出）② Bias-Effect Ratio (BER) — the auxiliary metric (introduced by this paper)
严谨定义：系统偏倚与校准后残留效应的大小之比（无界）： Formal definition: the ratio of systematic bias to the calibrated residual effect (unbounded):
BER = |μ̂B| / |ψ̃| = BF / (1 − BF)  ∈ [0, ∞)
⚖️ 通俗解释（倍数比喻）："偏倚是残留效应的多少倍"。BER = 9.6 读作"偏倚大约是残留效应的十倍"。倍数的好处是直观，坏处是当残留效应趋近 0 时它会爆炸（甚至 ∞），所以 v34 起只作辅助展示，分区判断全部交给有界的 BF。v35 模拟进一步给出了直接证据：在 48 个真实效应恰为零的条件（BFtrue = 1）下，BER 的相对偏倚根本无定义（真值是 ∞），而 BF 仍可正常估计（平均相对偏倚 −30.2%、RMSE 0.34，因 [0,1] 上限效应而向下收缩）——这正是论文选择 BF 作主指标的核心理由之一。 ⚖️ Plain language (multiple analogy): "how many times larger is the bias than the residual effect". BER = 9.6 reads as "bias is roughly ten times the residual effect". Multiples are intuitive, but they explode (to ∞) as the residual effect approaches zero — so from v34 onward BER is an auxiliary display only, and all zone classification is left to the bounded BF. The v35 simulation provides direct evidence: across the 48 conditions where the true effect is exactly zero (BFtrue = 1), BER's relative bias is simply undefined (the true value is ∞), whereas BF remains estimable (mean relative bias −30.2%, RMSE 0.34, attenuated downward by the [0,1] ceiling) — one of the core reasons the paper adopts BF as the primary metric.
③ 观测偏倚分数 Observed Bias Fraction (OBF) —— 描述性对照③ Observed Bias Fraction (OBF) — a descriptive contrast
严谨定义：偏倚占未校准（观测）估计的份额： Formal definition: the bias's share of the uncalibrated (observed) estimate:
OBF = |μ̂B| / |ψ̂obs|  ∈ [0, ∞)
🔎 通俗解释："论文里报告的那个 RR 里，偏倚占几成"。β 受体阻滞剂病例：OBF = 0.190 / 0.184 ≈ 1.03——估计出的偏倚比整个观测关联还大一点，即"那个 0.83 的 RR 几乎可以被偏倚全盘解释"。OBF 无界，仅作描述。 🔎 Plain language: "what share of the RR reported in the paper is bias". Beta-blocker case: OBF = 0.190 / 0.184 ≈ 1.03 — the estimated bias is slightly larger than the entire observed association, i.e., "the RR of 0.83 can be almost fully explained by bias". OBF is unbounded and purely descriptive.
④ 校准 p 值 Calibrated p-value④ Calibrated p-value
严谨定义：把观测估计对照经验零分布（而非标准正态）计算的双侧 p 值，检验"校准后效应是否可区分于零"。p > 0.05 = 扣除偏倚后无证据支持真实效应；p < 0.05 = 扣除偏倚后仍有可区分的效应。 Formal definition: a two-sided p-value computed against the empirical null distribution (not the standard normal), testing "is the calibrated effect distinguishable from zero". p > 0.05 = no evidence of a real effect after removing bias; p < 0.05 = a distinguishable effect remains after removing bias.
🚦 通俗解释：它是一个二元开关——只回答"扣除偏倚后是否有证据支持真实效应"。BF 则是一个连续刻度，量化偏倚占校准后信号的比例。两者互补：论文应同时报告。 🚦 Plain language: it is a binary switch — it only answers "is there evidence of a real effect after bias removal". BF is a continuous gauge quantifying bias's share of the calibrated signal. The two are complementary: papers should report both.
⑤ 三区分类 Three-zone classification⑤ Three-zone classification
严谨定义：基于 BF（等价于 BER）的启发式分区，CI 跨区时取更谨慎的一侧： Formal definition: a heuristic zoning based on BF (equivalently BER); when the CI spans zones, take the more cautious side:
效应主导 effect-dominated：BF < 1/3（残留效应 ≥ 偏倚 2 倍）Effect-dominated: BF < 1/3 (residual effect ≥ 2× bias) 混合区 mixed：1/3 ≤ BF ≤ 0.5（偏倚与效应混在一起，难分彼此）Mixed: 1/3 ≤ BF ≤ 0.5 (bias and effect intertwined) 偏倚主导 bias-dominated：BF > 0.5Bias-dominated: BF > 0.5
🎯 通俗解释（三色仪表盘）：绿灯区（效应主导）"信号盖过噪声，可谈疗效"；黄灯区（混合）"偏倚与效应混在一起、势均力敌，必须谨慎 + 做敏感性分析"；红灯区（偏倚主导）"这份数据不足以支持因果结论"。注意：红灯 ≠ 药物无效，只是"这份数据里分不清效应与偏倚"。 🎯 Plain language (three-color dashboard): green (effect-dominated) — "signal outweighs noise, efficacy can be discussed"; amber (mixed) — "bias and effect are intertwined and evenly matched; proceed with caution + sensitivity analyses"; red (bias-dominated) — "these data cannot support a causal conclusion". Note: red ≠ the drug is ineffective; it only means "these data cannot separate effect from bias".
⑥ Fieller 置信集 Fieller confidence set⑥ Fieller confidence set
严谨定义：对带符号比值 ρ = μ̂B/ψ̃ 的置信集，由不等式 (m₁−ρm₂)² ≤ z²α/2(v₁₁−2ρv₁₂+ρ²v₂₂) 定义。当分母 ψ̃ 太小时，它会诚实地退化为外集、空集或全直线。 Formal definition: a confidence set for the signed ratio ρ = μ̂B/ψ̃, defined by the inequality (m₁−ρm₂)² ≤ z²α/2(v₁₁−2ρv₁₂+ρ²v₂₂). When the denominator ψ̃ is too small, it honestly degenerates into an exterior set, the empty set, or the whole line.
🪞 通俗解释：普通区间在分母趋近 0 时可能给出误导性的有限区间；Fieller 集则对该比值给出诚实的置信区域——该多宽就多宽，该无界就无界。β 受体阻滞剂病例的 Fieller 集为 (−∞, −1.62]∪[1.45, ∞)，意思是"比值只能排除中间一小段，其余全都与数据相容"——这正是"校准后效应≈0"时该有的诚实回答。 🪞 Plain language: a conventional interval can mislead with a spuriously finite interval when the denominator nears zero; the Fieller set gives the ratio an honest confidence region — as wide or as unbounded as it needs to be. In the beta-blocker case the Fieller set is (−∞, −1.62]∪[1.45, ∞), meaning "only a small middle segment of ratio values is excluded; everything else is compatible with the data" — exactly the honest answer when the calibrated effect ≈ 0.
⑦ bootstrap 中位数点估计 Bootstrap-median point estimate⑦ Bootstrap-median point estimate
严谨定义：在 logit（BF）/ log（BER）尺度取 bootstrap 分布的中位数再反变换。点估计与置信区间出自同一个分布，因此点估计不可能落在自己的 CI 之外。 Formal definition: the median of the bootstrap distribution on the logit (BF) / log (BER) scale, back-transformed. The point estimate and the CI come from the same distribution, so the point estimate can never fall outside its own CI.
🔧 通俗解释：v33 曾出现过"点估计 413.2 > 自己的 CI 上限 204.8"的怪象（原始比值与 bootstrap 分布脱节）。改用中位数后，这种自相矛盾从机制上被消除。 🔧 Plain language: v33 once produced the oddity "point estimate 413.2 > its own CI upper limit 204.8" (the raw ratio detached from the bootstrap distribution). Switching to the median eliminates this self-contradiction by construction.
三区阈值为什么是 1/3 和 1/2？它们对应"残留效应 ≥ 偏倚的 2 倍"与"偏倚 ≥ 残留效应"两个可理解的界限（在 BER 尺度即 0.5 与 1）。这是启发式而非决策理论推导；且刻意不对称——观察性研究中误认因果的代价 > 过度谨慎的代价，所以红灯区留得更宽。 Why thresholds at 1/3 and 1/2? They correspond to the interpretable boundaries "residual effect ≥ 2× the bias" and "bias ≥ residual effect" (0.5 and 1 on the BER scale). This is a heuristic, not a decision-theoretic derivation; and it is deliberately asymmetric — in observational research the cost of a false causal claim exceeds the cost of excess caution, so the red zone is kept wider.

⑥ TTE研究案例（MIMIC-IV）⑥ TTE case study (MIMIC-IV) The beta-blocker TTE case study

在心衰（射血分数下降型，HFrEF）住院患者中比较"达到 ≥50% 目标剂量 β 受体阻滞剂"与"<50%"。本文中的这项研究案例在TTE设计实施与因果推论分析两个层面都使用了当前的"最佳实践"——严格遵循 TARGET 指南（Transparent Reporting of Observational Studies Emulating a Target Trial，目标试验模拟的报告规范）进行设计与报告，并采用TMLE——因果推断方法论前沿的双重稳健估计器进行分析。 The paper's case study (MIMIC-IV): among hospitalized heart-failure patients, comparing "achieving ≥50% of the target beta-blocker dose" with "<50%". The case study followed current best practice at both the design and analysis stages — it was designed and reported under the TARGET guideline (Transparent Reporting of Observational Studies Emulating a Target Trial) and analyzed with TMLE, a state-of-the-art doubly robust estimator at the frontier of causal inference methodology.

先厘清两个关键概念。
① "达到 ≥50% 目标剂量 β 受体阻滞剂" 与 "<50%" 分别指什么？ 心衰指南要求 β 阻滞剂从极小剂量起步、逐步向上滴定到"目标剂量"（例如比索洛尔 10 mg/天、卡维地洛 25 mg 每日两次、琥珀酸美托洛尔 200 mg/天）。本研究不比较"有没有开这个药"，而是比较"出院时实际被滴定到了多少"达到至少一半目标剂量的患者，与低于一半（<50%）的患者，并假设他们在出院后一年内维持出院时的用量。比较他们 1 年后的心血管事件发生率。这正是最容易混进健康依从者效应的地方：能顺利爬到目标剂量的，往往是更耐受、更自律、被更积极随访管理的人，他们本来预后就更好。
② "GDMT 治疗"是什么？为什么只算 3 个药、不是临床医生印象里的 4 个？ GDMT（guideline-directed medical therapy，指南导向的药物治疗）是心衰（射血分数下降型，HFrEF）的标准治疗。临床医生如今熟悉的"四根支柱"是：肾素-血管紧张素系统阻断剂（ACEI/ARB/ARNI）+ β 阻滞剂 + 盐皮质激素受体拮抗剂（MRA，如螺内酯）+ SGLT2 抑制剂（如达格列净）。但本研究只取前 3 类（ACEI/ARB/ARNI + β 阻滞剂 + MRA），不把 SGLT2 抑制剂算进去。原因是 SGLT2 抑制剂用于心衰是 2020–2021 年才写进指南的（达格列净的 EMPEROR-Reduced、恩格列净的 EMPULSE 研究），而 MIMIC-IV 这批患者的住院时间大多在这之前——队列里几乎没人用过 SGLT2 抑制剂。如果硬要"集齐 4 个药才算 GDMT"，我们的阳性对照人群会几乎为空、毫无区分度。所以本研究把 GDMT 锚定在当时真正的标准治疗上：3 类药物。这样 GDMT 才是一个"确实被广泛使用、且真实效应很大"的阳性对照，用来检验校准方法会不会矫枉过正（把真效应也一起扣掉）。 Two key concepts to clarify first.
① What do "achieving ≥50% of the target beta-blocker dose" and "<50%" mean? Heart-failure guidelines start beta-blockers at a minimal dose and titrate up step by step to a "target dose" (e.g., bisoprolol 10 mg/day, carvedilol 25 mg twice daily, metoprolol succinate 200 mg/day). This study does not compare "was the drug prescribed," but "how far was it actually titrated after discharge" — patients who reached at least half the target dose versus those below half (<50%), assuming they maintained their discharge dosage over the year after discharge. We compare their 1-year cardiovascular event rate. This is exactly where the healthy-adherer effect sneaks in: those who successfully climb to target dose are the more tolerant, more adherent, more actively managed patients, who already had a better prognosis.
② What is "GDMT therapy," and why only 3 drugs instead of the 4 clinicians expect? GDMT (guideline-directed medical therapy) is the standard treatment for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF). The "four pillars" clinicians now know are: renin-angiotensin system blocker (ACEI/ARB/ARNI) + beta-blocker + mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist (MRA, e.g., spironolactone) + SGLT2 inhibitor (e.g., dapagliflozin). But this study takes only the first three classes (ACEI/ARB/ARNI + beta-blocker + MRA), excluding the SGLT2 inhibitor. The reason: SGLT2 inhibitors entered heart-failure guidelines only in 2020–2021 (dapagliflozin's EMPEROR-Reduced, empagliflozin's EMPULSE trials), whereas most MIMIC-IV hospitalizations predate that window — almost no patient in the cohort was on an SGLT2 inhibitor. Insisting on "all 4 drugs = GDMT" would leave our positive-control group nearly empty and uninformative. So the study anchors GDMT to what was genuinely standard of care then: three drug classes. That makes GDMT a positive control that was "actually widely used and has a large true effect," used to test whether calibration over-corrects (removing the true effect along with the bias).

为什么做这项案例研究？——先说临床问题本身Why this case study? — start with the clinical question itself

β 受体阻滞剂是射血分数降低型心衰治疗的基石：随机试验（MERIT-HF、CIBIS-II、COPERNICUS）证明目标剂量能降低死亡。但真实世界里，能达到目标剂量的患者不到一半。于是每个心内科医生都见过这样的数据——"剂量达标的患者，死亡率更低"：观察性研究一再重复这个保护性关联，临床上的直接推论就是"应该更积极地向上滴定"。可问题在于：这是药的作用，还是人的作用？能被滴定到目标剂量的患者，本来就是血流动力学更稳、肾功能更好、依从性更高的患者（第①节的"健康依从者效应"）。这个问题无法用随机试验回答——伦理上不允许把患者随机分配到"不滴定"组——所以只能靠观察性数据，而观察性数据又天然被这个问题困扰。它是"表面关联与真实效应难以区分"的最典型场景，也是示范一个偏倚诊断指标的最佳考题。 Beta-blockers are a cornerstone of therapy for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction: randomized trials (MERIT-HF, CIBIS-II, COPERNICUS) proved that target doses reduce mortality. Yet in the real world, fewer than half of patients ever reach the target dose. So every cardiologist has seen the same data — "patients who reach the target dose have lower mortality": observational studies reproduce this protective association again and again, and the clinical implication seems to be "titrate more aggressively." The problem: is it the drug, or the patient? Patients who can be titrated to target are, to begin with, the hemodynamically stabler, renal-function-better, more adherent patients (the healthy adherer effect of Section ①). A randomized trial cannot answer this — one cannot ethically randomize patients to "no titration" — so only observational data can speak, and observational data are exactly where this problem lives. It is the canonical case of "observed association vs. real effect," and therefore the ideal exam question for demonstrating a bias diagnostic.

为什么选 MIMIC-IV 这个数据库？三个理由。① 真实的混杂结构：这是真实 ICU 的真实临床数据——混杂以它真实的样子存在，而不是教科书里的玩具数据；指标若在这里失灵，就是真的失灵。② 出院后死亡随访完整：MIMIC-IV 的死亡记录延伸到出院之后，"1 年全因死亡"这一硬终点才有完整随访（许多数据库做不到，只能用住院死亡等软性替代）。③ 完全公开、可复现：任何人都能下载数据、重跑整个分析——方法学示范的必要条件；配套的 ECG 数据还提供了 QRS、QTc 等生理协变量。最终队列：14,677 例心衰住院患者。 Why the MIMIC-IV database? Three reasons. ① A real confounding structure: these are real clinical data from a real ICU — confounding exists in its true form, not a textbook toy; if the metric fails here, it truly fails. ② Complete post-discharge mortality follow-up: MIMIC-IV's date-of-death records extend beyond discharge, so the hard endpoint of 1-year all-cause mortality has complete follow-up (many databases cannot do this and must fall back on in-hospital death). ③ Fully public and reproducible: anyone can download the data and rerun the entire analysis — a necessary condition for a methods demonstration; the accompanying ECG data also supply physiological covariates such as QRS and QTc. Final cohort: 14,677 heart-failure hospitalizations.

Any bias-removal method carries an intrinsic risk: overcorrection — removing the real effect along with the bias. How do we know it didn't? Run an exposure known to work through the identical pipeline. The mortality benefit of GDMT (guideline-directed medical therapy, achieving ≥2 of 3 classes) is repeatedly confirmed by randomized trials; if this calibration erased GDMT's benefit too, it would be overcorrecting. The result: the beta-blocker protective association (RR 0.83) vanished after calibration (RR 1.01), while GDMT's benefit (RR 0.65) remained significant after calibration (RR 0.78) — same method, same controls, same database: "what should be removed was removed; what should survive survived." This is exactly the clinical-lab practice of spike recovery: add a known amount of analyte to the sample and check whether recovery is near 100%, verifying the assay system is not systematically distorted.
先看这个案例有多"严谨"，再看它输出了什么：这项病例研究按当前标准几乎无可挑剔——设计层面严格遵循 TARGET 指南（资格标准、治疗策略、分配、随访、结局、因果对比全部预先明确，完整模拟目标随机试验）；分析层面采用 TMLE，因果推断方法论前沿的双重稳健估计器，配合 SuperLearner 与 10 个基线协变量。然而校准诊断显示：即便有这双重保障，估计仍被系统性偏移主导（BF = 0.91，校准后 RR = 1.01）。两层含义：其一，"偏倚主导"的判决不可能被归咎于分析粗糙——它出自一个全流程规范的严格研究；其二，方法论上的最优配置并不能消除未测量混杂——设计规范与估计精良各自对付的是它们设计上能解决的威胁，经验性偏倚诊断不是对研究严谨性的否定，而是最佳实践的必要补充。 First see how rigorous this case study is, then see what it produced: by current standards the study is close to impeccable — at the design level it strictly follows the TARGET guideline (eligibility, treatment strategy, assignment, follow-up, outcome, and causal contrast all specified up front, fully emulating the target randomized trial); at the analysis level it uses TMLE, a state-of-the-art doubly robust estimator at the frontier of causal inference, with SuperLearner and 10 baseline covariates. Yet the calibration diagnostic shows: even under this dual safeguard, the estimate remained dominated by systematic shifting (BF = 0.91, calibrated RR = 1.01). Two lessons: first, the "bias-dominated" verdict cannot be attributed to a sloppy analysis — it came from a rigorous, protocol-conformant study; second, the best available methodology cannot remove unmeasured confounding — rigorous design and sophisticated estimation each address the threats they were built to address, and an empirical bias diagnostic is not a concession against rigor but a necessary complement to best practice.

为什么用 TMLE 双重稳健估计器，以及为什么用 SuperLearner 集成配置？Why a doubly robust TMLE estimator, and why a SuperLearner ensemble configuration? Why TMLE + SuperLearner

本研究在分析层采用 TMLE（targeted maximum likelihood estimation，靶向最大似然估计）配合 SuperLearner 集成学习。这不是随意选择，而是为了让"规范设计（TARGET）"与"前沿估计"各司其职、互相补位。 At the analysis layer the study uses TMLE (targeted maximum likelihood estimation) with a SuperLearner ensemble. This is not arbitrary: it lets rigorous design (TARGET) and frontier estimation play complementary roles.

① TMLE 的双重稳健性（doubly robust）。TMLE 同时拟合倾向模型与结果模型，并把两者结合起来。它的关键性质是：只要两者之一设定正确，因果估计就一致。相比之下，传统回归调整要求结果模型完全正确、IPTW（逆概率加权）要求倾向模型完全正确，任一误设即偏。TMLE 还是半参数有效的，在同类估计器中方差最小。因此即便真实关系复杂，TMLE 也不像单一模型那样脆弱。 ① TMLE is doubly robust. TMLE fits both a propensity model and an outcome model and combines them. The key property: the causal estimate is consistent if either one is correctly specified. By contrast, plain regression requires the outcome model to be exactly right, and IPTW requires the propensity model to be exactly right; misspecify either and it biases. TMLE is also semiparametrically efficient, attaining the smallest variance among its class. So it is far less fragile than a single model.

② SuperLearner 是 TMLE 的"配置"层（nuisance 引擎）。TMLE 的质量取决于那两个 nuisance 函数的拟合。SuperLearner 不赌单一参数形式，而是用交叉验证从一组候选算法（glm、gam、xgboost 等）中按样本外风险选出最优加权集成。即使真实关系高度非线性，集成也能自适应捕捉；渐近上它不劣于（且通常优于）任一单个候选。本研究测试了三套库（SL.glm / +SL.gam / +SL.xgboost），结论稳健（见第⑥节敏感性检验）。 ② SuperLearner is TMLE's configuration layer (nuisance engine). TMLE's quality hinges on those two nuisance functions. SuperLearner does not bet on one parametric form; via cross-validation it selects the optimal weighted ensemble from candidate algorithms (glm, gam, xgboost, etc.) by out-of-sample risk. Even if the true relation is highly nonlinear, the ensemble adapts; asymptotically it is no worse (usually better) than any single candidate. We tested three libraries (SL.glm / +SL.gam / +SL.xgboost); conclusions were stable (Section ⑥ sensitivity checks).

为什么还需要一项模拟研究？——因为真实数据上没有"标准答案"Why also a simulation? — because real data come with no answer key

上一节的案例研究有一个无法回避的盲点：在真实数据上，没有人知道正确答案。MIMIC-IV 里 β 受体阻滞剂的真实效应到底是多少？不知道——所以当 BF 判定"偏倚主导"时，严格说我们无法对答案。一个新指标要让人敢用，必须先在已知答案的场合验证过。 The case study above has an unavoidable blind spot: on real data, nobody knows the right answer. What is the true beta-blocker effect in MIMIC-IV? Unknown — so when BF rules "bias-dominated," there is strictly no answer key to check against. Before a new metric can be trusted, it must first be validated where the answer is known.

这在检验医学里是常识：一个新试剂用于临床之前，必须先用已知浓度的标准品做分析性能验证——灵敏度、特异性、抗干扰——然后才允许检测未知样品。模拟研究就是 BF 的分析性能验证。做法：数据由我们自己生成，所以真实效应（ψ）和真实偏倚（μB）由我们自己设定——标准答案掌握在出题人手里；然后让 BF 走完它在真实数据上要走的全部流程（拟合经验零 → 算 BF → 三区分类），对照标准答案打分。 This is common sense in laboratory medicine: before a new assay enters clinical use, it must first be validated against reference samples of known concentration — sensitivity, specificity, interference — and only then applied to unknown specimens. The simulation is exactly that analytical validation for BF. The data are generated by us, so the true effect (ψ) and the true bias (μB) are set by us — the answer key is in the examiner's hands. BF is then run through the identical pipeline it faces on real data (fit the empirical null → compute BF → three-zone classification) and graded against the key.

两项研究各证明一件事，缺一不可：案例研究证明这个指标"有用"——在真实临床数据上落地、结论对分析设定稳健（第⑥节）；模拟研究证明它"可靠"——在已知答案的考题上判得准，并诚实暴露自己的失效边界（本节）。只做案例研究，指标可能是"碰巧"判对；只做模拟研究，指标可能适应不了真实数据的复杂性。 The two studies prove different things, and both are needed: the case study proves the metric is useful — it runs on real clinical data and its verdict is robust to analysis specifications (Section ⑥); the simulation proves it is reliable — it grades correctly on questions with known answers and honestly exposes where it fails (this section). With only the case study, the metric might have judged right by luck; with only the simulation, it might not survive the complexity of real data.
结论速览：靠得住——但只在其承诺的范围内。模拟（336 条件 × 200 次重复 = 67,200 次分析）显示：BF 分类在两端可靠（红灯区 80.0%、绿灯区 95.1%），在中间不可靠（黄灯混合区仅 41.8%，仅略高于随机猜测（33.3%）。因此 v34 的规则是：两端可以下结论，中间诚实地说"分不清"——改用"CI 完全落进某区才判定"的选择性规则后，凡是能下结论的，正确率 86.8%（但只覆盖 47.6% 的情形）。换句话说：它能可靠地认出"明显偏倚主导"和"明显效应主导"，对"中间地带"不强行判定。 Bottom line: it works — within what it promises. The simulation (336 conditions × 200 replications = 67,200 analyses) shows BF classification is reliable at the extremes (red 80.0%, green 95.1%) and unreliable in the middle (amber mixed zone only 41.8%, barely above the 33.3% chance level). Hence the v34 rule: conclude at the extremes, honestly say "can't tell" in the middle — under the selective rule "classify only when the whole CI falls inside one zone", decisive calls are 86.8% accurate (but cover only 47.6% of cases). In other words: it reliably recognizes "clearly bias-dominated" and "clearly effect-dominated", and refuses to force a verdict on the middle ground.

四步读法A four-step reading A four-step reading

  1. 看负对照的分布特征：负对照是否整体偏向一个方向？（是 → 系统偏倚存在；否或双向 → 谨慎，μ̂B 可能被稀释。）Inspect the negative-control pattern: do the controls lean in one direction overall? (Yes → systematic bias exists; no or both directions → caution, μ̂B may be diluted.)
  2. 看校准后效应与校准 p：校准后效应还剩多少？是否可区分于零？（校准 p > 0.05 = 扣除偏倚后无证据支持真实效应；校准 p < 0.05 = 仍有可区分的效应。）Check the calibrated effect and calibrated p: how much effect survives, and is it distinguishable from zero? (calibrated p > 0.05 = no evidence of a real effect after bias removal; p < 0.05 = a distinguishable effect remains.)
  3. 看 BF 及其 CI：CI 完全在红灯区（BF>0.5）→ 不能作因果解读；完全在绿灯区（BF<1/3）→ 信号盖过偏倚；跨区/黄灯 → 证据不足，不强行判定。Read BF with its CI: CI entirely in the red zone (BF>0.5) → no causal interpretation; entirely in the green zone (BF<1/3) → signal outweighs bias; straddling zones or amber → insufficient evidence, don't force a verdict.
  4. 看敏感性：留一法、模型设定、宽限期是否改变分区；Fieller 集是否无界（分母太小时必须诚实）。Check sensitivity: do leave-one-out, model specification, or grace-period changes alter the zone; is the Fieller set unbounded (mandatory honesty when the denominator is small).

常见误读清单Misreadings to avoid Misreadings to avoid

✗ "BF = 0.91 说明效应只剩 9%。"错。BF 的"份"是偏倚与残留效应两者合计的份，不是对真实效应的估计。
✗ "红灯区 = 药物无效。"错。红灯 = "这份观测数据不足以支持因果结论"。
✗ "BF 接近 1，所以偏倚精确占 97%。"错。接近 1 是"饱和"信号，数值只作数量级读。
✗ "黄灯（混合区）说明偏倚与效应真的各占一半。"错。黄灯 = "分不清"，不是"相等"。 ✗ "BF = 0.91 means only 9% of the effect remains." Wrong. BF's "share" is of bias plus residual effect combined, not an estimate of the true effect.
✗ "The red zone means the drug is ineffective." Wrong. Red = "these observational data cannot support a causal conclusion".
✗ "BF near 1 means bias is precisely 97%." Wrong. Near 1 is a "saturation" signal; read it as an order of magnitude.
✗ "Amber (mixed) means bias and effect are truly half-half." Wrong. Amber = "can't tell", not "equal".
